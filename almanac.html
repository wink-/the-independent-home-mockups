A cyclical view of independence

What does this home need this season?

Instead of a reverse-chron blog, the site is filed like a farmer's almanac: power, food, water, money, and privacy tasks arrive when the season actually calls for them.

Every entry still starts on paper. The scan stays attached. But the reader enters through the year — not the latest post — and leaves with one timely job, not ten tabs.
