Dispatch 14 — the solar year, honestly

One year of panels, written out the way the invoices came in.

Posted Sep 22, 2026 · from the property First class · one page, both sides
We are one year into the panels. The array made 8.9 MWh this year, which covered 71% of what the house used. The other 29% came off the grid, almost all of it in January when the trees win. It cost $2,940 out of pocket after the credit. I know that number because I wrote down every invoice, including the two I would rather not have. If the house can make power and cost less to run, then work gets a little less powerful over my life. That is the whole plan, and it is not a secret one.
the credit took 11 weeks — worth it, but plan for it
P.S. I am not a prepper. I just want more control.
