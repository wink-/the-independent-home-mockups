Design rule

Paper remains the proof. The blueprint is the interface.

The strongest current direction is still handwritten-first. This divergent mockup keeps that trust model but changes the wrapper: each notebook entry becomes a service bulletin, parts list, revision note, or operating procedure.

Readers can skim the technical plate for the practical answer, then open the original handwritten scan when they want the human source.
