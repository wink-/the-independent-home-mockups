The proofThe same run, printed twice

Orange — what went right
  • Solar covered 71% of house use by August, in its first full year.
  • Shop time doubled once the bench got a top flat enough to trust.
  • First side-income month cleared $1,000 after parts.
  • Two of six garden beds paid for themselves in groceries.
Green — what it cost
  • $2,940 net out of pocket after the credit. Not free. Not close.
  • Two weekends lost to a permit office, one to a wrong-size disconnect.
  • Three jobs mispriced. The free redo taught more than the profit would have.
  • Six months of evenings. The calendar does not care that it was worth it.

Both columns set at the same weight, same measure. Neither one is the footnote.
