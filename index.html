Nine directions

Handwritten-first site concepts.

Built around the idea that every post starts on paper, the full original scan stays available, and AI only transcribes, structures, and publishes.

My recommendation

Keep Field Journal as the base; borrow Ledger and Workshop modules.

The Field Journal version still best sells the authenticity idea. Ledger gives the recurring “monthly control ledger” component. Workshop gives the cleanest path to lead magnets and paid products without making the site feel scammy.

Original four

1. Field Journal

Most distinctive. Rugged notebook, paper-first, typed monospaced transcript.

recommended base

2. Independence Ledger

Dashboard-first. Best for numbers, W2 optional progress, monthly reports.

best component library

3. Homestead Magazine

Warmest and broadest. Good for mainstream readers and long-form stories.

best general audience

4. Terminal Paper Lab

Most tech-forward. Good for AI/terminal/streetcats overlap.

best experimental angle
New task-built directions

5. Ephemera Board

Kitchen-table clippings, receipts, hand notes, and proof scraps. Makes the raw source material feel collectible and trustworthy.

best authenticity texture

6. Home Systems Blueprint

Technical-manual view of power, water, food, money, and privacy as house systems you can inspect and improve.

best systems map

7. Workshop Manual

Course and lead-magnet direction: practical modules, checklists, and starter paths without hype or guru energy.

best product funnel

8. Home Almanac

Seasonal filing by power, food, water, money, and privacy. Reader enters through the year, not the feed.

best timely structure
Ninth direction / my entry

9. Proof Sheets

Two-colour risograph field zine. Orange prints what went right, green prints what it cost, so the mistakes and the real numbers get the same press run as the wins. Drag the registration control to pull the two ink plates together.
