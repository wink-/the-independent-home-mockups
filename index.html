Seven directions

Handwritten-first site concepts.

Built around the idea that every post starts on paper, the full original scan stays available, and AI only transcribes, structures, and publishes.

My recommendation

Keep Field Journal as the base; borrow Ledger and Workshop modules.

The Field Journal version still best sells the authenticity idea. Ledger gives the recurring “monthly control ledger” component. Workshop gives the cleanest path to lead magnets and paid products without making the site feel scammy.
